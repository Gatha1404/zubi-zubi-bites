How We Make Non-Gluten Flours Melt-In-Your-Mouth 🔬

Non-gluten grains usually crumble easily. Here is our 3-step kitchen technology for perfect crispy panda shapes!

1

Hot Water Scalding

Boiling hot water gelatinizes tapioca & native grain starches, turning fragile flour into a smooth, flexible dough without gluten.

2

Kadhai Salt-Baking

Cooked on induction at 165°C using salt-bed heat circulation. Zero deep-frying, preserving low-oil counts and maximum crunch.

3

Post-Bake Dusting

Immediate light oil mist spray onto warm bites followed by fine seasoning dust (Peri-Peri, Magic Masala) for instant stickiness.
